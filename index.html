ISHimulation: Mapping Zebrafish Single-Cell Gene Expression to Embryonic Anatomy

Single-cell RNA sequencing identifies which zebrafish cell populations express a gene, but tissue dissociation removes information about where those cells were located in the embryo. ISHimulation is a proposed research and teaching resource that includes an interactive web tool for projecting Daniocell cluster-level expression data onto predefined regions of a schematic zebrafish embryo. The tool visualizes two measures of expression for each region: the fraction of cells with detectable transcripts and relative mean expression.

In a proof-of-concept evaluation, expression maps for 10 genes across segmentation, pharyngula, and hatching stages were compared with observed whole-mount in situ hybridization (ISH) patterns from the Thisse collection curated in ZFIN. Of 24 scored gene–stage pairs, 9 were concordant, 7 partially concordant, and 8 discordant. The mean Jaccard index was 0.49. The maps captured tissue-level patterns such as pronephric cdh17 and notochordal tbxta expression. Discrepancies for pax2a and egr2b illustrated the difficulty of resolving discrete neural domains from broad tissue clusters. Limited transcript detection may have contributed to discrepancies for crx and early egr2b, while ambient RNA may have contributed to unexpected krt4 signal.

ISHimulation provides a way to compare single-cell expression summaries with observed embryonic expression patterns, identify discrepancies for follow-up, and demonstrate how cluster resolution and transcript detection shape the anatomical patterns that can be recovered.

Approach 2: landmark mapping

Worth it only for the brain and neural tube, where ISH patterns are finer than any cluster. It won't fix genes Daniocell barely detects, and it needs a curated landmark-gene panel for each stage.

Approach 3: spatial-reference mapping

Not worth it yet. It fixes the same resolution misses as approach 2 at much higher cost, and only for stages a spatial atlas covers. Revisit if approach 1b still misses along the head-to-tail axis.

Methods and caveats

  • Predicted. For each gene, Daniocell's "all cells" dot plot was parsed (dot size → % cells expressing; dot color → mean expression on that gene's own color scale, rescaled 0–1, approximate). Each tissue group was assigned to one cartoon region. A region is called when any of its groups has ≥ % of cells expressing and ≥ relative expression.
  • Observed. ZFIN wild-type expression annotations from the Thisse high-throughput ISH screens (ZDB-PUB-010810-1, -040907-1, -051025-1, -080227-22), grouped into Thisse stage bins and mapped to the same regions.
  • Stage matching. Segmentation = Daniocell 14–21 hpf vs Thisse 14–19 somites. Pharyngula = 24–34 hpf vs 20–25 somites to Prim-25. Hatching = 48–58 hpf vs High-pec to Long-pec (42–48 hpf, so slightly earlier).
  • "No ISH data" means Thisse annotated nothing for that gene at that stage. That isn't proof of absence, so those cells aren't scored.
  • Known biases. Ambient RNA (e.g., krt4 shows up at low levels in almost every cluster), dissociation loss, dropout of low-expressed genes, incomplete ISH annotation (e.g., pax2a pronephros isn't annotated at 14–19 somites), and colors read approximately from plots rather than raw counts.
